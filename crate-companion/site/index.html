Crate Companion

Your collection, beautifully at hand.

Crate Companion is a companion app for Discogs collectors of records, CDs and cassettes. Browse your shelves, identify an album from a photo, check whether you already own a pressing, and find what's for sale from the shops you trust.

  • Your Discogs collection and wantlist, with every cover and image full screen.
  • Photograph a sleeve, label or runout to find the exact pressing.
  • Recommendations and liner-style notes, with AI included.
  • Check your favorite shops for albums on your wantlist.

Crate Companion is an independent app. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Discogs.
