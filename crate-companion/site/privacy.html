Crate Companion

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Crate Companion is a companion app for record, CD and cassette collectors who use Discogs. It's built to keep your information on your phone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing about you is ever sold or shared for marketing.

In short: your Discogs sign-in stays on your phone. The app talks to Discogs to show your collection. When you use an AI feature, only what that feature needs is sent to Anthropic's Claude through our AI server, which keeps usage counts but not your photos, requests or answers.

Your Discogs account

When you connect with Discogs (or enter a personal access token), the sign-in credentials are stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. The app uses them to talk directly to Discogs: to show your collection, wantlist and marketplace prices, and to make the changes you ask for, such as adding an album or rating one. We never receive your Discogs credentials. What Discogs does with your account is covered by the Discogs Privacy Policy.

AI features

Some features use Anthropic's Claude: identifying an album from photos, recommendations, album notes, reviewing marketplace listings and picking something to play. They run only when you tap them. Each one sends only what it needs, such as:

  • the photos you take or choose to identify an album (the app shrinks them first);
  • your request text, and album titles, artists and ratings from your collection and wantlist; and
  • public Discogs details about albums and listings.

These requests go through our AI server (run on Cloudflare) to Anthropic, which returns the answer. Our server passes them along and doesn't keep your photos, requests or answers. Anthropic handles them under its commercial terms and privacy policy; it doesn't use them to train its models.

To keep the free allowance fair, the AI server keeps:

  • A random install ID the app creates the first time it runs. It isn't tied to your name, Discogs account or device. It's used to count your monthly uses.
  • Usage counts per install per month, and the month's total cost.
  • A daily count per network, to stop abuse. Your IP address is never stored: only a one-way hash made with a random key that's replaced every day, so it can't be traced back to an address.

Shared album notes. Notes the AI writes about an album are saved by Discogs release number and shown to everyone who opens that release, so they're only written once. They contain only facts about the album, and nothing about you or your collection.

Your own key. If you add your own Anthropic API key in Settings, it's stored only on your phone and AI requests go directly from the app to Anthropic, not through our server.

Like any website, Cloudflare may briefly keep standard request logs (including IP addresses) for security and reliability, under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph an album to identify it. Photos are sent for identification as described above and aren't saved by the app or our server.

Favorite shops and settings

Your favorite Discogs shops, preferences and cached album data are stored only on your phone.

Deleting your information

Disconnecting in Settings removes your Discogs sign-in from the phone. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Usage counts on the AI server can't be linked to you, and they're no longer used once the month ends. If you'd like us to delete them anyway, email us your install ID (shown in Settings) and we'll remove them.

Children

Crate Companion isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Everything the app sends travels encrypted over HTTPS.
