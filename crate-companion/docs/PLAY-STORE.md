**Full description** (4000 max)

    Crate Companion is a beautiful companion app for Discogs collectors of vinyl records, CDs and cassettes. Sign in with your Discogs account and your whole collection is in your pocket, with the artwork front and center.

    YOUR COLLECTION, LOOKING ITS BEST
    • Browse your collection and wantlist as a wall of covers or a tidy list, and filter by folder, style and format.
    • Every album gets a rich page: the cover with the record, CD or cassette sliding out behind it, your rating and condition, the track listing, credits, runout etchings and barcodes.
    • Tap the cover to see every image of the release full screen: front, back, labels and inserts. Pinch to zoom in on the details.
    • An album of the day from your own shelves, and the value of your collection.

    IN THE RECORD STORE
    • Check an album: type a catalog number, scan the barcode or photograph the cover, label or runout to find the exact pressing in your hand.
    • See instantly whether you already own it or want it, and whether the price is fair against the cheapest copy on Discogs.
    • Add it to your collection or wantlist with one tap.

    FIND WHAT'S NEXT
    • Favorite shops: list the Discogs sellers you trust, set the grades and price you'll accept, and see which albums on your wantlist they have.
    • Recommendations from your own taste, or ask for something specific in your own words.

    AI INCLUDED
    Identify albums from photos, get recommendations, liner-style notes on any album, a second opinion on marketplace listings, and something from your shelves to play tonight. A monthly allowance of AI uses comes with the app, with no account or key needed. Power users can add their own Anthropic API key.

    PRIVATE BY DESIGN
    Your Discogs sign-in stays on your phone. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

    Crate Companion is an independent app for Discogs users. It isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Discogs. A free Discogs account is required.
